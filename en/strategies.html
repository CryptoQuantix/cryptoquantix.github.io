Core Holding

Macro Core

A trend-following strategy designed to capture major market cycles. It enters long positions when the price crosses above the 200-day Simple Moving Average (SMA200d) and manages risk using a Chandelier exit set at 5x the 20-day Average True Range (ATR20d).

  • Active only on BTC (ETH rejected during validation)
  • Volatility-targeted sizing (30%)
  • +315% over 4 years (Max Drawdown: 24.7%)

Execution Logic

Entry Condition Price > SMA(200d)
Exit Condition Price < Chandelier(5xATR20d)
Trade Frequency ~2 trades / year

Trend Breakdown

A highly effective breakout/breakdown system that adapts to the macro regime. It shorts the breakdown of the 48-hour low during a macro BEAR market, and longs the breakout of the 7-day high during a macro BULL market.

  • No Take Profit — fixed 7-day holding period
  • Short Profit Factor: 1.26 (123 trades)
  • Long Profit Factor: 1.53 (84 trades)

Execution Logic

Macro Gate (Long) BULL (Price > SMA200d)
Macro Gate (Short) BEAR (Price < SMA200d)
Exit Condition Time-based (168 hours)
Contrarian

Funding Squeeze

A rare but highly profitable contrarian strategy. It triggers short entries when the perpetual funding rate hits its cap while the 200-day SMA is actively declining, signaling a "deep-bear capitulation" event.

  • Active on both BTC and ETH
  • BTC Profit Factor: 2.65
  • Captures extreme market dislocations

Execution Logic

Funding Condition Funding Rate == Cap
Trend Condition SMA200d Declining
Trade Frequency ~3-4 trades / year
