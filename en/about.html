The Quant Paradigm Shift

CryptoQuantix was born from a fundamental realization: intraday orderflow in crypto is largely noise. After years of testing volume breakouts, mean reversions, and liquidity squeezes, the data spoke clearly — those strategies lacked a persistent statistical edge.

The June 2026 Pivot

In June 2026, we executed a complete architectural pivot. All intraday strategies were disabled and archived. We shifted our focus entirely to macro-regime quantitative trading. By zooming out to multi-day and multi-week timeframes, we found robust inefficiencies that survive across different market cycles.

Why We Are Different

  • No Curve Fitting: We test across 4 full years (June 2022 to June 2026), capturing deep bear markets, explosive bull runs, and choppy transitions. If a strategy doesn't survive all three, it's discarded.
  • Strict Macro-Gating: Our strategies are structurally turned off during adverse conditions. For example, the Trend Breakdown long side will never fire during a macro bear regime (defined by the 200-day SMA).
  • Portfolio-First Risk: We don't just manage trade risk; we manage portfolio risk. Our 1.5x Gross Exposure cap ensures the bot never over-leverages, even if multiple strategies trigger simultaneously.

Open Source & Transparent

We believe in radical transparency. The entire trading engine, execution logic, and dashboard are open source. The historical research reports, documenting both our successes and our failed intraday attempts, are available in the repository.
